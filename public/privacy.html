Privacy policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

What we collect

Your name, email address and handle; the password you choose (stored only as a salted hash) or, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID and confirmed email address; the skills you upload; the organizations you join and the access levels set for you; and a log of calls your agents make through services your organization shares.

Sign in with Google

When you sign in with Google we ask only for your name, email address and Google account ID, to confirm the address is yours. We do not read your Gmail, Calendar or Drive through this sign-in, and we do not store Google access tokens from it.

How we use it

Only to run Rucksack: signing you in, syncing skills to your agents, enforcing the access your organization gives you, and billing. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Admins of an organization you join can see your name, email, access levels and the calls your agents made with that organization’s services. Skills are shown only to the people you share them with. Payments are handled by Stripe.

Keys your organization stores

API keys an organization adds are encrypted at rest with a key unique to that organization and are used only to make the calls its members are allowed to make.

Deleting your data

Email us and we will delete your account and the skills you own.
